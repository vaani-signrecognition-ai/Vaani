About VAANI

VAANI is a platform built to empower the deaf and speech-impaired community by transforming sign language into voice and text using AI. We believe every hand deserves to be heard.

Empowering Through Sign Language

VAANI, which means "Voice" in Hindi, is a pioneering initiative dedicated to promoting Indian Sign Language (ISL) accessibility and education. Founded with the vision of creating an inclusive society, we bridge the communication gap between the deaf and hearing communities.

Our platform leverages cutting-edge technology to provide interactive learning experiences, real-time feedback, and comprehensive resources for mastering ISL. We collaborate with NGOs, educational institutions, and government bodies to make sign language education accessible to everyone.

Our Commitment:
  • Promote Indian Sign Language as the primary mode of communication for the deaf community
  • Provide accessible and engaging learning resources
  • Foster inclusivity through technology and community engagement
  • Support deaf empowerment and employment opportunities

Our Vision

We envision an inclusive world where communication has no barriers, where sign language is universally recognized, and every individual can connect, express, and be understood with dignity.

Creating an Inclusive Society

We envision a world where Indian Sign Language is universally recognized, respected, and integrated into all aspects of society. Our vision extends beyond language education to create a truly inclusive environment where every individual, regardless of hearing ability, can communicate freely and participate fully in social, educational, and professional spheres.

Educational Integration

Make ISL a standard part of school curricula nationwide

Professional Inclusion

Create employment opportunities through sign language proficiency

Universal Accessibility

Ensure all public services are accessible through ISL

Community Building

Foster stronger connections between deaf and hearing communities

Building Bridges Through Communication

Our mission is to democratize Indian Sign Language education through innovative technology, community engagement, and strategic partnerships. We are committed to breaking down communication barriers and empowering both the deaf community and hearing individuals to connect, learn, and grow together.

Core Objectives:
1
Develop Accessible Learning Tools

Create user-friendly platforms and resources that make ISL learning engaging and effective for people of all ages and backgrounds

2
Promote ISL Standardization

Work with linguistic experts and the deaf community to establish and promote standardized Indian Sign Language

3
Foster Community Engagement

Organize workshops, events, and awareness campaigns to bring together deaf and hearing communities

4
Advocate for Policy Changes

Collaborate with government bodies to implement policies that recognize and support sign language rights

5
Provide Professional Development

Offer certification programs and training for educators, interpreters, and professionals working with the deaf community
