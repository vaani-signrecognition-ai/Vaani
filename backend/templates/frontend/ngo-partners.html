ISLRTC

Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre

National institute for Indian Sign Language research and training under DEPwD, Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India.

New Delhi, India

National Association of the Deaf

Advocacy and support organization

Working towards rights, education, and empowerment of deaf individuals in India through advocacy, awareness, and community programs.

Multiple locations across India

www.nadindia.org

All India Federation of the Deaf

Federation of deaf schools and organizations

Promoting sign language education and accessibility across educational institutions, with membership from schools and organizations nationwide.

New Delhi, India

www.aifd.org.in

Hearing Hope Foundation

Support organization for deaf children

Supporting deaf children and their families with resources, early intervention programs, and educational support across rural and urban areas.

Mumbai, India

www.hearinghopefoundation.org

Promoting digital accessibility and inclusive technology solutions for people with disabilities, including deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals.

Bangalore, India

www.accessibilityindia.org
